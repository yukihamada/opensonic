Planet-Scale Streaming

From 2 to 3 billion listeners.

Auto-scaling 3-tier cascade + P2P swarm.

🌎

3-Tier Cascade Relay

Origin (1 node) → Region (~20 nodes) → Edge (~10,000 nodes). Up to 300K listeners per node.

🤖

P2P Swarm

Listeners automatically become micro-relays. Fan-out 4, depth 16 = 4.2 billion nodes. Near-zero server bandwidth.

📦

P2P File Distribution

BitTorrent-style track delivery. After everyone downloads, synced playback with zero bandwidth.

🔗

FILE_URL Distribution

Just share a CDN URL. Each listener downloads + SYNC:play for perfect sync. Zero DJ bandwidth.

⚡

sendmmsg() Batch Sending

1024 packets per syscall on Linux Edge. Can saturate 10GbE NIC. Worker thread pool support.

📋

Auto Tier Selection

2-4 people: direct P2P. 5-50: single relay. 50-100K: 2-tier. 100K+: full 3-tier cascade.

Copyright & Royalty

Use music right. Pay artists fairly.

Async copyright detection with zero audio quality sacrifice + real-time billing.

🎵

Zero-Latency Detection

Spectral fingerprinting runs asynchronously on a separate thread. try_lock ensures the audio pipeline is never blocked.

💰

DJs pay. Listeners are free.

Only DJs playing copyrighted music on public channels are billed. No ads, no subscriptions.

📈

Tiered Pricing

Volume-based discounts by listener count. 5-person party: $0.015/track. 1M-person live: $224/track.

🔒

Private = No Charge

Private mode, LAN, and direct P2P connections are exempt from copyright detection. Home BGM is completely free.

🎬

Licensed Tracks One-Click

JASRAC/CC/original tracks ship instantly via LICENSED_PLAY. No detection, no charge.

💲

Wallet & Economy

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Soluna free? +
Yes. Soluna is open source (MIT License) and completely free. There are no software usage or licensing fees. Royalties only apply when streaming copyrighted music on public channels (private use is free).
How do you handle copyright? +
Soluna uses spectral fingerprinting technology to automatically detect tracks and distribute royalties to rights holders. Processing runs on a separate thread from the audio pipeline, so there's no impact on audio quality or latency. Revenue is split: 70% rights holders, 10% platform, 20% DJ cashback.
Do I need a Mac to listen on iPhone? +
No Mac is needed for receiving (RX) only. Just enter a channel name and receive audio from anywhere via the WAN relay. A Mac + solunad is required on the transmitting (TX) side.
What's the latency like? +
Under 20ms on LAN (nearly real-time). Typically 50-100ms via WAN relay. Auto delay sync ensures all speakers play sound at the same moment.
What platforms are supported? +
Transmit (TX): macOS 12 and later. Receive (RX): iOS 16+, Android 8.0+, Raspberry Pi / Linux, browser (WebSocket). Windows is in Beta.
Does it really scale to 3 billion? +
The architecture combines a 3-tier cascade relay (Origin → Region → Edge) with a P2P swarm (fan-out 4, depth 16 = 4.2 billion nodes). Since listeners themselves become micro-relays, server bandwidth stays near zero. Theoretically supports 3 billion+ listeners.
