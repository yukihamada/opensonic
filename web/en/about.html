Thesis

Reinventing Music Experience Infrastructure

SOLUNA is not just another music streaming service. It is a project to replace every layer between where sound is made and where it is heard — streaming, sync, payments, and rights management — with an open-source protocol.

Where Spotify and Apple Music treat music as "files to distribute," SOLUNA is a model for sharing the music experience itself in real time.

Possibilities

A Map of What's Possible

EVENT x TECHNOLOGY

Hybrid Festivals

1,000 people at a physical venue + 100,000 online sharing the same DJ set with zero latency. P2P swarm delivery makes distribution costs near zero. Run a globally synced festival across Tokyo, LA, and Berlin simultaneously. Anyone with a phone can open a SOLUNA channel and turn a bar, beach, or park into a satellite venue instantly.

ARTIST ECONOMY

Beyond Subscriptions: Monetizing DJs

Instead of Spotify's $0.003/play, SOLUNA offers real-time royalties based on listeners x time. If 10,000 people listen for an hour, rights holders get paid on the spot. Curation — track selection and mixing — becomes directly monetizable through three tiers: tips, support, and channel subscriptions. Artists just open a SOLUNA channel and streaming, billing, and rights management are all handled.

DAILY AUDIO INFRA

Everyday Audio Infrastructure

Sync every speaker in your home with under 5ms latency — an open-source, free alternative to Sonos and AirPlay. Venues can open a SOLUNA channel with automatic copyright detection and billing, eliminating manual licensing paperwork. Anyone can launch a global "radio station" where listeners power the distribution via P2P — zero server costs to reach a million people.

DISTRIBUTED DETECTION

Listeners Power Copyright Protection

Traditional music detection requires expensive centralized servers. SOLUNA flips this model: every listener device generates an audio fingerprint from the music it is already playing, and submits it to a consensus algorithm. When multiple listeners report the same fingerprint, the track is confirmed and royalties flow automatically. More listeners means higher accuracy — copyright protection that scales with the audience itself.

PLATFORM STRATEGY

Hardware Integration & Platform Growth

Open source means zero adoption barriers. Speaker and DJ hardware makers can implement the SOLUNA protocol natively. Integration with Pioneer DJ and Denon lets DJs broadcast directly to the SOLUNA network from their gear. Through CarPlay and Android Auto, drivers can join real-time channels on the road.

BUSINESS MODEL

A Sustainable Revenue Structure

A transparent 10% platform fee on all transactions, maximizing the share that goes to DJs and artists. Additional revenue from annual custom channel name sales, SOLUNA infrastructure licensing for events, and a certification program for SOLUNA-compatible speakers.
