Soluna Rx receives high-quality network audio streams over your local network using RTP multicast.

Features:
- Receive audio from Soluna transmitters on your local network
- Support for both OSTP and AES67 audio protocols
- Adaptive jitter buffer with automatic health management
- Remote speaker control via WebSocket
- Bonjour auto-discovery of nearby Soluna daemons
- Multi-speaker synchronization with latency measurement
- Packet loss concealment (PLC) for smooth playback
- Real-time statistics: packets, drops, buffer, concealment

Perfect for multi-room audio setups, professional audio monitoring, and synchronized playback across devices.

Requires a Soluna-compatible audio transmitter on the same network.
