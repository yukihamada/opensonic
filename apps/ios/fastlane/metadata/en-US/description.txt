Soluna Rx turns your iPhone into a high-quality WiFi audio receiver. Stream 24-bit/48kHz audio from your Mac to any speaker system over your local WiFi network.

Features:
• Lossless 24-bit audio streaming over WiFi
• Ultra-low latency with adaptive jitter buffering
• Forward Error Correction (FEC) for reliable playback
• WSOLA packet loss concealment for seamless audio
• Auto-detection of WiFi vs wired network
• Multi-sample-rate support (44.1/48/96 kHz)
• Web-based control interface
• Works with Raspberry Pi + DAC for audiophile setups

Perfect for whole-home audio, studio monitoring, and audiophile listening rooms. Simply run the Soluna transmitter on your Mac and receive pristine audio on any iOS device.
